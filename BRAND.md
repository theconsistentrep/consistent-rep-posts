# The Consistent Rep: brand and posting rules

Instagram: @theconsistentrep (Windsor connector `instagram`, account id `17841439110991118`)
Public image base: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/theconsistentrep/consistent-rep-posts/main/

## Voice
- First person, honest, warm, no hype. The page documents a real woman's training and consistency, not a guru.
- Share what *I* do ("my leg day", "what keeps me going"), never prescribe ("you must", "do this to lose 10kg").
- No nutrition prescriptions, no calorie targets, no medical or supplement claims, no before/after promises.
- English. Short lines. One clear idea per post.

## Look
- Background #16161A (charcoal), text #F4EFE8 (cream), accent #FF6B3D (orange), muted #A7A29A.
- Fonts: Poppins ExtraBold for headlines, Inter for body and labels.
- Soft orange radial glow, outlined orange numbers for list items, short orange bar under text.
- Carousel 1080x1350 JPEG (templates/carousel.py). Reel 1080x1920 MP4, ~10s, still scenes with 0.25s fades, silent AAC track (templates/reel.py).
- Keep text away from the bottom ~500px and right edge on Reels (Instagram UI).

## Content pillars (rotate)
1. Consistency habits and mindset (carousel or text Reel)
2. "My workout today": exercises from my own sessions, framed as what I did
3. Myths and lessons learned in training (framed as personal experience)
4. Motivation quotes in brand style
5. Soft call-to-action about once in 5 posts: follow, save, share, comment a number

## Captions
- Hook line, 2–4 short lines, a question to drive comments, "Save this" or "Share with your gym buddy".
- 8–12 hashtags mixing broad and niche: #consistency #fitnessjourney #gymmotivation #womenwholift #glutetraining #gymhabits #disciplineovermotivation #lisbonfitness #theconsistentrep
